The deposit number is what the depleted graft is short of.  Leaving
it out of the readout makes the load respond to the carrier count far
more steeply than it does, because the mass is then spread thin over a
deposit population the operator assumes is still there, and the
threshold cuts it twice.  Putting it in gives an exponent close to the
measured one and leaves the seeding bottleneck intact, now for a
physical reason rather than an assumed threshold: with inert carriers
there are no centres for the amyloid to collect on, so what little
forms is spread over specks too small to resolve.
