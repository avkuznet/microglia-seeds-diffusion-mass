  Every graft-level panel in d'Errico et al. is "one graft from n mice", so
  all of these standard errors are clustered.  rho = 1 above assumes grafts
  within a mouse are perfectly correlated, which BOUNDS the inflation rather
  than estimating it; the conclusion is unchanged either way.  The published
  ED Fig. 1c test, F(2,28), has 31 observations, so it is graft-level too.

  The log scale is the one quoted in the paper.  On it the measured
  4-to-16 week increase is not itself significant and its interval
  contains the model value, so the apparent discrepancy in the ratio of
  means is an artefact of assuming normality for a skewed quantity.
